Company Overview

Providing Industrial Excellence Since 1985

Founded by Jayprakash Vasa & Girish Vasa, Jinharsh has grown into a leading industrial partner distributing top-tier power transmission, bearings, and lubrication solutions across India.

Four Decades of Engineering Legacy

Starting in 1985 with Timken distribution, Jinharsh expanded its footprint to become an authorized channel partner for over 24 global leaders including FAG, Rexnord, NTN, Dodge, Rollix, Groz, and Shell.
