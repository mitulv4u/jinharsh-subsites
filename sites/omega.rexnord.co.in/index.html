Popular Applications for OMEGA Couplings

Non-lubricated, torsionally soft performance built to protect connected bearings, seals, and gearboxes.

01 · Fluid Handling

Centrifugal & Process Pumps

Type ES spacer couplings allow maintenance teams to replace pump mechanical seals without disturbing motor alignment. Split flex element installs in minutes.

02 · Air & Gas

Compressors & Blowers

Torsionally soft urethane cushions pulsating torque and vibration in rotary screw, lobe, and reciprocating compressor drives.

03 · HVAC & Power

Industrial Fans & Cooling Towers

High angular and parallel misalignment capacity accommodates thermal growth and structural flex in forced-draft fans and cooling towers.

04 · Bulk Handling

Conveyors, Crushers & Screens

HDY Heavy-Duty Yellow elements absorb severe start-stop shock loads on belt conveyors, bucket elevators, vibrating screens, and steel mill runout tables.

05 · Process Industries

Mixers, Agitators & Extruders

Operates in horizontal or vertical orientations without extra components—ideal for chemical reactors, pulp & paper agitators, and plastic extruders.

06 · Harsh Environments

Water, Wastewater & Chemical Plants

HSU (Green) hydrolytically stable urethane resists moisture, heat, and caustic or acidic washdowns where standard rubber fails.
