How It Splits & Installs

Radial Split-in-Half Element & Reversible Hubs

The OMEGA flex element is split radially into two half-shells (like clam-shells around the shaft). You bolt each half radially to the reversible hubs—no axial shaft movement and no motor realignment needed.

Step 1 · Mount Reversible Hubs

Mount hubs Inboard (IN) or Outbound (OUT) per Page 6/8 drawings. Same hubs fit E & ES.

Step 2 · Align Shafts

Align shafts per Manual 80152. Weight-balanced halves meet ISO G16 (1504 RPM) & AGMA Class 8.

Step 3 · Bolt First Half-Shell

Wrap first half around hubs and thread radial cap screws with thread-locking adhesive (do not lubricate threads).

Step 4 · Rotate 180° & Torque

Rotate shaft 180°, mount second half-shell, and torque cap screws to spec. Install OSHA guard.

Material Engineering

What is OMEGA Made Of?

Unlike ordinary rubber tire couplings, Rexnord OMEGA uses a specially formulated cross-linked polyurethane chemically bonded directly to machined carbon or stainless steel shoes.

Standard Orange Polyurethane: -40°C to +93°C, high fatigue resistance & chemical durability.
HDY Heavy-Duty Yellow: +25% torque & +40% torsional stiffness.
HSU Green (Hydrolytically Stable): Built for hot, humid, steam, caustic & acidic plants.

Rexnord OMEGA vs. Normal Rubber Tire Coupling

Feature Rexnord OMEGA® Normal Rubber Tire Coupling
Element-to-Hub Grip Polyurethane-to-metal bond (zero slip) Friction-clamped rubber beads (can slip)
Replacement Time Split-in-half radial bolts (~10 mins, no realignment) Full clamping ring disassembly & re-torque
Torque Density Higher peak torque lets you pick a smaller coupling Lower rubber shear strength requires larger OD
Oil & Moisture Life Resists industrial oils, ozone & humidity (HSU) Natural rubber swells/cracks in oil & heat
